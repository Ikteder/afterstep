## Implementation observations

- Separating `game.js` from rendering made complete route verification possible without browser automation.
- Echoes hold their last position after their recorded route ends. This makes short committed routes useful instead of requiring filler waits.
- Sigils accept committed echoes only. Letting the active player energize a sigil would allow several levels to bypass the time-loop mechanic.
- Entity overlap is intentional and documented; walls provide the spatial constraints.
- Responsive browser QA exposed a transient zero-width Canvas resize that produced a negative rounded-corner radius. Rendering now skips transient sub-100-pixel dimensions and clamps the helper radius to a non-negative value.
